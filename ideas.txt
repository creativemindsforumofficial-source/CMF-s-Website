# Creative Minds' Forum (CMF) Project Blueprint & Rules

## Core Architectural Principle
- **Multi-Page Architecture (MPA)**:
  - The CMF website is an expanding multi-page platform with dedicated, independent pages for each creative domain and initiative.
  - Distinct routes for:
    - `/` -> Home
    - `/about` -> About CMF, Vision & Pillars
    - `/my-pen-speaks` -> Creative Archive & Anonymous Submissions
    - `/blog` & `/blog/:slug` -> Editorial Insights & Thought Leadership
    - `/team` -> Global Team Roster
    - `/newsletter` -> CMF Dispatch & Community Announcements
    - `/sub-teams` -> Specialized Guilds & Applications
    - `/ai-symposium` -> Global AI & Creative Think-Tank
    - `/audacity` -> Flagship Annual Conference
    - `*` -> 404 Lost in Creative Space (NotFound)

## Responsiveness & Device Standards
- **Mandatory Mobile & Tablet-First Optimization**:
  - Every update, new component, and new page MUST be thoroughly optimized for mobile (320px–640px) and tablet (768px–1024px) viewports before completion.
  - Card carousels must support fluid touch swiping and horizontal scrolling.
  - No unintended horizontal body overflow.
  - Touch-accessible interactive elements (minimum 44px tap targets).
  - Multi-page scroll resets cleanly to top on every route change.
